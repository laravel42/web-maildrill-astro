Multichannel does not mean three disconnected tools. It means one audience, channel-specific content, and a clear reason for each touch.

## Start with the job

Define the outcome — activate, convert, retain — then assign the channel that fits urgency and richness. Email for depth, SMS for immediacy, WhatsApp for conversation.

## Share the audience

Lists and segments should live once. When email and SMS target different CRMs, suppression and consent become guesswork.

## Keep the wizard honest

Maildrill's five-step wizard forces sender, audience, content, review, and schedule in that order so teams do not skip readiness checks.
