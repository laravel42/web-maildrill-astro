A campaign that never reaches the inbox is a campaign that never happened. Before you scale volume, walk this checklist.

## Authenticate

SPF, DKIM, and DMARC should be green before the first marketing send. Maildrill surfaces domain status in Settings → Domains so the team does not guess.

## Warm deliberately

Ramp volume from known-engaged contacts. Avoid blasting a cold list on day one — reputation compounds slowly and breaks quickly.

## Monitor placement

Watch bounce, complaint, and open trends after every send. Campaign detail KPIs exist so you can catch drift early.

## Recover fast

Suppress hard bounces, honor unsubscribes immediately, and fix content that trips spam scoring before the next batch.
