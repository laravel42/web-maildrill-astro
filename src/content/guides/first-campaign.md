Open Campaigns → New campaign and follow the wizard.

1. **Sender** — pick the channel and verified identity.
2. **Audience** — choose lists and saved segments; confirm the recipient count.
3. **Content** — compose with the channel-aware editor and live preview.
4. **Review** — run spam and domain readiness checks.
5. **Schedule** — send now or pick a window.

When the batch completes, open the campaign drawer for the six-KPI grid.
