About — Maildrill, the workspace for every message
/ About

We make messaging feel effortless.

Maildrill started with a simple frustration: sending a campaign meant juggling a stack of disconnected tools, each with its own audience, its own dashboard, its own quirks. We thought running campaigns should feel like running campaigns — not operating software.

/ Mission

One workspace, one audience, every channel.

We're on a mission to unify email, SMS, WhatsApp, and voice into a single, fast workspace — where a marketer builds a segment once and reaches people wherever they are, without exports or reconciliation.

Speed, clarity, confidence, and scale — in that order. Every decision we make starts by asking whether it helps someone ship a better campaign, faster.
