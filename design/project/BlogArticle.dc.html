Voice is here: automated calls join Maildrill

TW Tom Whitfield · Co-founder & CTO Jul 8, 2026 · 4 min
04

Today we're adding voice to Maildrill. Email, SMS, and WhatsApp now share the workspace with automated calls and IVR — all driven by the same audience, segments, and analytics you already use.

For most of our history we said no to voice. It felt like a different category — different vendors, different compliance, a different mental model. But the requests never stopped, and they all rhymed: the message that finally lands isn't always a message at all. It's a call.

Why a fourth channel

Some moments demand a phone call. An appointment nobody confirmed. A payment that's about to lapse. A delivery that needs a signature. For these, an unread email or a muted text isn't good enough — and a human calling one by one doesn't scale.

  • Reach the unreachable. Voice cuts through when other channels are ignored.
  • Interactive by design. IVR lets people confirm, reschedule, or route themselves with a keypress.
  • Naturally a fallback. No answer? Fall back to SMS or WhatsApp automatically.

One audience, still

The point of Maildrill was never "more channels." It's one audience across every channel. Voice plugs into the same contacts and live segments as your email and messaging — so a journey can start with an email, follow up over SMS, and place a call when it matters, measured as a single funnel.

"The message that finally lands isn't always a message. Sometimes it's a call."

Available today

Voice is live on every account now — text-to-speech in 30+ voices, IVR flows, call tracking, and channel fallbacks. Setup takes a few minutes. Read the Voice overview or jump straight into your workspace.
